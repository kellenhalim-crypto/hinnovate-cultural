BEYOND THE VIRAL MOMENT

Understanding the influence of trends on a generation.

01 — THE BIGGER PICTURE

More Than Just Trends

Viral trends are more than just popular videos, challenges, or online activities. They reflect how teenagers communicate, express themselves, discover new interests, and connect with different cultures.

Social media allows trends to spread quickly, influencing the way teenagers think, behave, and interact with the world around them.

02 — WHAT HAVE WE LEARNED?

Understanding the Influence

Through our research, we discovered how social media, teenagers, and culture are connected through viral trends.

Social Media

Social media helps trends reach millions of people and become popular within a short time.

Teenagers

Teenagers participate in trends to have fun, express themselves, and feel connected to others.

Culture

Music, fashion, food, and traditions influence trends and allow different cultures to be shared.

03 — OUR FINAL MESSAGE

Enjoy Trends, Make Your Own Choices

Following trends isn't necessarily a bad thing. What matters is understanding why we follow them and recognising their influence on our decisions.

We should be able to enjoy trends, explore new experiences, and appreciate different cultures without feeling pressured to follow everything that becomes popular.

THE MESSAGE TO TAKE AWAY

"Don't just follow the trend. Understand the influence behind it."

Be inspired by trends, connected through culture,
but never lose your individuality.
