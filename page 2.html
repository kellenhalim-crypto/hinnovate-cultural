About Our Hinnovate Project

PAGE 02
We are Group 1 (Kellen, Riri, Mexi, and Aksa). Our core focus is investigating how cultural expression adapts and spreads as viral trends in this rapid digital era. Trends shape what teenagers see, share, and mimic daily—but we want to analyze the deep mechanisms driving why these trends become so massive.

Our Objectives

What we want to discover through our project.

Understanding Teenagers 01 / UNDERSTAND
We analyze why teenagers follow trends and want to be part of what's popular. By studying what motivates their decisions, we aim to map out general behavioral patterns among youth.
Exploring Social Media 02 / EXPLORE
We investigate how platforms, creative algorithms, and peer circles accelerate trends instantly, looking closely at how online spaces control daily interactions.
Discovering Culture 03 / DISCOVER
We track how music, fashion, food, traditions, and language cross over into digital spaces. We want to see how culture inspires trends and how trends introduce audiences to new heritages.
Identifying the Impact 04 / IDENTIFY
We map how digital fads alter teenagers' real-life habits, behaviors, and long-term perspectives, keeping a balanced view on both the positive updates and negative side effects.

  • S_02:
