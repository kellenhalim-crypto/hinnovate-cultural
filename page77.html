How Trends Influence Teenagers

Viral trends don't just influence what teenagers watch or participate in. They can also shape their interests, communication, decisions, and everydaay lifestyles.These influences can bring both positive and negative changes.

01 / Behaviour & Lifestyle

Trends can influence teenagers' daily habits, activities, and lifestyle choices

Example: Trying a new hobby, changing fashion preferences, or adopting popular routines.

02 / Communication & Language

Social media introduce new slang, expressions, and ways of communicating.

Example: Teenagers start using viral phrases or internet slang in evryday conversations like "slay" or "pick me"

03 / Interests & Hobbies

Viral contents can introduce teenagers to activities and interests they might never have discovered otherwise.

Example: Discovering photography, cooking, sports, or music through social media.

04 / Identity & Self-Expressions

Trends can influence how teenagers express their personality, creativity, and interests. They can also inspire teenagers to experiment with different styles that goes viral in social media.

Example: Exploring different fashion styles or creative ways to express themselves.
