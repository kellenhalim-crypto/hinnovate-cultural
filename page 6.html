Culture Goes Viral

PAGE 06

Culture plays an important role in shaping viral trends. Through social media, cultural elements such as traditional food, music, fashion, and language can reach people across the world. At the same time, viral trends can introduce teenagers to cultures they might never have experienced before.


Culture Inspires Trends
Cultural traditions, food, music, fashion, and language can become the inspiration behind viral trends. People share these cultural elements online, allowing others to discover and recreate them.
Trends Spread Culture
Social media helps cultural elements travel beyond their countries of origin. As teenagers participate in viral trends, they can introduce others to traditions, customs, and lifestyles from different cultures.
Culture Evolves Through Trends
When cultural elements become popular online, people sometimes adapt them into modern styles or combine them with influences from other cultures. This creates new ways for younger generations to express and experience culture.
