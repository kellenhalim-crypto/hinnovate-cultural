Conclusion Based on the Interviews

In today’s digital era, teenagers from Grades 7 to 12 spend a significant portion of their daily lives online, especially on social media platforms like TikTok. While online platforms provide entertainment and a way to connect with peers, they also expose students to viral online trends and social pressures. This study was conducted by Group 1 to examine the screen-time habits of junior and senior high school students, explore whether feelings of exclusion or the desire for attention influence their online behavior, and understand what actions parents take to manage their screen time.

Interview from G7 - G12



Grade 7's results

Average screen time: 6 hours
Most G7 students have felt excluded from friends or family at some point, yet almost none of them wanted to seek attention or felt pressured to fit in. Very few students in G7 have tried an online trend compared to higher grade levels. In their free time, their main activities are scrolling TikTok and playing online games like Mobile Legends (ML). For the majority of G7 students, parents have not taken any strict actions to limit their daily screen time.

G8's results

Average screen time: 7.5 hours
All interviewed students in G8 have felt excluded from friends or family, yet none of them wanted to seek attention. However, all of them have tried online trends (such as "velocity" and "blind deaf mute"). Their main free-time activity is scrolling TikTok, alongside reading books.

G9's results In

Average screen time: 8 hours
Summary: Most G9 students have felt excluded, every student wanted to get attention, and all of them have tried an online trend. In their free time, they usually do hobbies, play on their phones, or text friends. Most parents took action to limit screen time by taking away their phones.

G 10's results

Average screen time: 9.5 hours
Summary: All interviewed students in G10 have felt excluded, every student wanted to get attention, and all of them have tried an online trend. During their free time, they usually bake or scroll TikTok. Most parents limit their screen time by telling them to do other activities like playing music or eating.

G 11's results

Average screen time: 6.3 hours
Summary: Most G11 students have felt excluded, every student wanted to get attention, and all of them have tried an online trend. Scrolling TikTok is the most common free-time activity, followed by sleeping or reading. For most students, parents took no action to limit their screen time, except for one whose phone was taken away.

G 12's results

Average screen time: 5.6 hours
Summary: All interviewed students in G12 have felt excluded, half of them wanted to get attention, and most have tried an online trend. Their free-time activities include sleeping, playing basketball, and scrolling TikTok. None of the G12 students had any parental restrictions to limit their screen time.
